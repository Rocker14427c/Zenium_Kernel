## Reading of the table

* **32 of 68 audited subsystems carry no ported content** - these are the vendor-only
  driver trees (`arm_display`, `cmdq5`, `ccci`, connac wireless, MTK ASoC, ...). They are
  not a regression from this port: 5.15 has no upstream equivalent, so each needs its own
  transplant + dependency fix, which is the device-driver work tracked in
  `MIGRATION-5.15.md`.
* Subsystems where 5.15 *has* a driver for this device's hardware (see `DT-bound dirs`)
  are the ones where the port preserves functionality through an upstream driver rather than
  the vendor one; where the column is empty, the DTB node is data without a consumer.
* `vendor hunks (manual)` is the residual: manual hunks are 4.19-shaped changes (removed APIs,
  renamed ops, vendor struct fields) that cannot be textually applied and were reviewed by
  hand rather than shipped blind.
